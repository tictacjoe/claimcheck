The Accountability Project — Reports

  • The Accountability Project — Overview

    The Accountability Project began in early 2025 as a personal reference archive of news reports about changes happening in the federal government. The flood of news made it difficult to keep up with the many impactful and concerning events being reported, and much was lost in the churn of the daily news cycle. As the volume and velocity of information increased, I began exploring ways to grow and manage this archive using AI-based tools.

  • The Accountability Project — Cabinet-Level Legal Exposure Update Sweeps

    A Cabinet-Level Legal Exposure Update Sweep is a systematic, multi-stage audit and verification pipeline designed to maintain high evidentiary standards across legal exposure records for federal officials. Because court proceedings, Inspector General investigations, congressional subpoenas, and agency actions evolve continuously, this sweep ensures that every statutory violation record remains accurate, corroborated, and legally sound.

  • The Accountability Project — Community Topics and Submission Sweeps

    A Community Topics and Submission Sweep is an interactive intake and verification workflow that bridges public oversight and rigorous data auditing. While the primary curated trackers focus strictly on named individual statutory liability, formal agency rule rollbacks, and institutional degradation, citizen readers frequently surface emerging policy questions, viral social media claims, and local impacts that merit rigorous, independent examination.

  • The Accountability Project — Corporate Deregulation Update Sweeps

    A Corporate Deregulation Update Sweep is a structured audit and verification workflow that tracks federal agency rule rollbacks, compliance stays, and statutory rollbacks across environmental, labor, consumer safety, and financial domains (EPA, OSHA, DOL, DOE, CFPB, FCC, DOT, MSHA, USDA, and other regulatory bodies).

  • The Accountability Project — Government Service Redirection Update Sweeps

    A Government Service Redirection (GSR) Update Sweep is a systematic review process that tracks the deliberate degradation of federal institutional capacity.
