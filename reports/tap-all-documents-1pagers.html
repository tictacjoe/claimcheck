1-Pager 1: TAP Master Project Overview

Source Document: docs/reports/tap-project-overview.md
Scope: Master Whitepaper & System Architecture
Core Purpose: Comprehensive introduction to TAP as a code-enforced intelligence archive for oversight staff, counsels, and researchers.

Executive Gist

The Accountability Project (TAP) solves the problem of modern 24-hour news churn, where high-impact administrative orders and regulatory actions vanish from public memory within days. TAP provides an auditable, anti-hallucinatory intelligence platform that replaces emotional rhetoric with verifiable statutory violations, primary docket citations, and quantified impact modeling across five specialized functional areas.

Key Pillars & Mechanisms

  1. The Five Functional Trackers: (1) Cabinet-Level Legal Exposure, (2) Corporate Deregulation, (3) Government Service Redirection, (4) Reporting Archive Ingestion, and (5) Community Topics Submissions.
  2. Code Over AI Gatekeeping: Generative AI is strictly constrained by schema validators, automated staleness triage scripts, and mandatory primary-source resolution (GAO decisions, IG audits, Federal Register dockets) before data ingestion.
  3. Dual-Repository Architecture: Maintains an air-gapped private research environment (tap-data) paired with a high-speed, zero-backend static web application (tap-site on GitHub Pages).

1-Pager 2: Cabinet-Level Legal Exposure System

Source Document: docs/reports/tap-sweep-cabinet-legal-exposure.md
Scope: Tracker Audit Workflow (194 Active Appointee Dossiers)
Core Purpose: Systematic tracking of individual political appointee statutory liability and paired debate preparation.

Executive Gist

Rather than assigning vague blame to an entire administration, this system enforces personal legal accountability. It maps the official actions of Cabinet secretaries and agency heads to explicit elements of federal statutes, establishing an unassailable factual record paired with anticipated opposing defenses and rock-solid comebacks.

Key Pillars & Mechanisms

  1. Statutory Core: Focuses on six key compliance frameworks: the Impoundment Control Act, Hatch Act, Antideficiency Act, Administrative Procedure Act, Federal Advisory Committee Act, and Federal Vacancies Reform Act.
  2. 4-Stage Update Cycle: (1) Automated staleness triage targeting active legal proceedings, (2) Primary-source docket and IG investigation research in 6-file batches, (3) Programmatic JSON updates with schema validation, and (4) Two-repo deployment.
  3. The 3-Tier Action Taxonomy: Classifies updates into Light Touch (procedural milestones), Moderate Updates (formal rulings), and Real Corrections (generating deep-dive research briefs).

1-Pager 3: Corporate Deregulation Tracking System

Source Document: docs/reports/tap-sweep-corporate-deregulation.md
Scope: Regulatory Rollback Workflow (56 Rulemaking Dossiers)
Core Purpose: Monitoring the systematic dismantling of environmental, labor, consumer, and financial safeguards.

Executive Gist

This system tracks federal regulatory rollbacks across agencies like the EPA, OSHA, DOL, CFPB, and USDA. It strictly separates the verifiable procedural status of a rule in the Federal Register from its modeled economic and health impacts, requiring all public harm estimates to cite established institutional models.

Key Pillars & Mechanisms

  1. Strict Data Requirements: Every entry must identify the named political proponent, corporate beneficiaries (tied to lobbying disclosures), formal Federal Register status, and quantified health/safety consequences.
  2. Model Attribution Standard: Prohibits unanchored casualty or cost claims; requires explicit attribution to named regulatory impact analyses (EPA RIA, NYU Policy Integrity, OSHA risk assessments).
  3. Structured Audit Pipeline: Enforces automated 80–110 character summary constraints and verifies that stay orders and injunctions reflect current appellate court dockets.

1-Pager 4: Government Services Redirection System

Source Document: docs/reports/tap-sweep-government-services-redirection.md
Scope: Institutional Capacity Tracker (257 Agency Dossiers)
Core Purpose: Documenting the degradation of nonpartisan federal agency operational capacity and public services.

Executive Gist

This tracker documents how federal agencies are systematically hollowed out or redirected away from their statutory missions. It categorizes institutional damage into five distinct patterns and requires all findings to be corroborated by independent oversight watchdogs or court orders.

Key Pillars & Mechanisms

  1. The 5 Impact Domains: (1) Research & science suppression, (2) Congressionally appropriated funding redirection, (3) Civil service workforce and office cuts, (4) Mandatory data collection halts, and (5) Public protection rollbacks.
  2. Evidentiary Hierarchy: High-confidence entries require formal GAO impoundment findings, Inspector General reports, or judicial preliminary injunctions.
  3. Fair Presentation & Scope: Requires quantified figures (personnel loss, dollars frozen) while presenting agency administrative rationales fairly in dedicated caveat fields.

1-Pager 5: Community Topics & Public Submissions System

Source Document: docs/reports/tap-sweep-community-topics-submissions.md
Scope: Intake Triage & Lightweight Fact-Checking
Core Purpose: Processing public suggestions and early-warning community concerns without diluting core evidentiary rigor.

Executive Gist

This workflow provides a structured gateway for crowdsourced topic submissions. It triages user suggestions into curated promotion candidate pools or publishes transparent, objective fact-checking verdicts while preserving the submitter's original phrasing verbatim.

Key Pillars & Mechanisms

  1. The 3 Triage Outcomes: (1) Promotion to Curated Tracker (if backed by a named official and statutory violation), (2) Lightweight Community Fact-Check, or (3) Polite off-scope rejection.
  2. 4-Tier Verdict Standard: Evaluates claims under four transparent ratings: confirmed, partially_confirmed, unable_to_verify, or misleading_as_stated.
  3. Cross-Tracker Navigation: Automatically generates jump links connecting community topics to related entries in the Cabinet, Deregulation, and Service Redirection datastores.

1-Pager 6: Reporting Backlog Mining & Triage System

Source Document: docs/reports/tap-sweep-reporting-backlog-triage.md
Scope: Data Mining & Semantic Promotion Pipeline
Core Purpose: Extracting verified investigative reporting patterns to feed the curated tracking databases.

Executive Gist

This pipeline bridges the high-volume reporting archive and the curated tracking databases. It applies automated semantic clustering and keyword heuristics across thousands of raw journalistic entries to group related events, eliminate duplicates, and prepare candidate batches for human verification.

Key Pillars & Mechanisms

  1. 5-Stage Ingestion Loop: (1) Candidate mining via regex/keywords, (2) Semantic topic clustering, (3) AI structured pre-pass, (4) Human-in-the-loop promotion decision, and (5) JSON compilation.
  2. Targeted Discovery: Scans for institutional triggers including subpoenas, grant freezes, inspector general removals, and regulatory stays.
  3. Quality Gate: Ensures raw news claims are never published directly to curated trackers without primary-source docket or Federal Register validation.

1-Pager 7: Reporting Database Automated Update Pipeline

Source Document: docs/reports/tap-sweep-reporting-database-updates.md
Scope: Automated Scraping & Archive Indexing Pipeline (13,200+ Entries)
Core Purpose: Zero-editorial mechanical ingestion of daily investigative journalism.

Executive Gist

This operational pipeline manages the automated daily scraping, parsing, and structured dataset generation of the external reporting archive. It provides a historical foundation of over 520 published reports and 13,200 individual entries without requiring editorial overhead.

Key Pillars & Mechanisms

  1. Single-Command Orchestration: Executed via check_for_new_posts.sh, running sequential Python workers (scrape_archive.py, parse_post.py, and build_dataset.py).
  2. Defensive Parsing: Handles HTML entity cleaning, metadata normalization, date standardizing, and deduplication against the master dataset index.
  3. Continuous Maintenance: Runs on automated schedules with error-logging to maintain an up-to-date historical backdrop for candidate mining.

1-Pager 8: Documentation Architecture & Web Publishing System

Source Document: docs/reports/tap-docs-audit-and-publishing-report.md
Scope: Publishing Engine & Markdown-to-HTML Compilation
Core Purpose: Converting internal system documentation into accessible, interactive public web pages.

Executive Gist

This system automates the verification, path sanitation, and web publication of all TAP internal documentation. It guarantees that process guides and whitepapers are accessible as clean static HTML on the public website with verified hyperlinks and consistent styling.

Key Pillars & Mechanisms

  1. Audit & Path Sanitization: Converts internal local file paths into relative web links, corrects schema ordering, and validates cross-tracker references.
  2. Automated Static Build (build_docs.py): Uses Python markdown with table, fence-code, and attribute extensions to compile Markdown source files into standalone HTML pages.
  3. Navigation Integration: Automatically generates navigation breadcrumbs, category indexes, and mobile-friendly responsive wrappers for the public site.
