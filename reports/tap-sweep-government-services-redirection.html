The Accountability Project — Government Service Redirection Update Sweeps

Concept & Purpose

A Government Service Redirection (GSR) Update Sweep is a systematic review process that tracks the deliberate degradation of federal institutional capacity.

Unlike Corporate Deregulation (which tracks public-facing industry rules) or Cabinet-Level Legal Exposure (which tracks individual criminal or statutory liability), GSR tracks actions where the machinery of government itself is suppressed, defunded, dismantled, or redirected across five distinct domains:

  • research_suppression: Halting, censoring, or delaying scientific research (e.g. NIH mRNA reviews, CDC measles forecast suppression).
  • funding_redirection: Unlawful impoundments, grant clawbacks, or cancelled agency contracts.
  • staff_office_cuts: Mass firings, forced resignations, office closures, and quorum-loss / vacancy crises.
  • data_collection_halted: Deleting federal datasets or cancelling recurring statistical surveys (e.g. USDA food security reports).
  • protection_rollback: Rescinding internal health and safety protections for populations under direct federal care (e.g. military vaccine mandates, VA healthcare directives).

Stage 1: Institutional Backlog & Staleness Detection

The sweep begins by running the GSR staleness scanner.tn

The script audits every JSON entry in the datastoretn and flags:

  • Unsettled Statuses: Entries whose status contains "ongoing", "pending", "unresolved", "unsettled", or "litigation".
  • Verification Age Check: Entries where last_verified is older than 60 days.

Stage 2: Watchdog, Institutional Record & Impact Research

Because TAP entries require a high level of empirical rigor, research is prioritized against authoritative institutional sources:

  • Priority Sourcing Hierarchy:
  • Formal Watchdog & Oversight Findings: Government Accountability Office (GAO) Impoundment Control Act legal decisions, Inspector General reports, and Federal Vacancies Reform Act rulings.
  • Named Proponents on Record: Verifying the specific official (e.g., OMB Director, agency secretary, or acting administrator) who signed the order or directive.
  • Quantified Institutional Impact: Documenting hard numbers (grant dollars withheld, employee headcounts reduced, or specific datasets removed) from authoritative sources.
  • Multi-Outlet Corroboration: Requiring independent reporting from two or more major news organizations before upgrading confidence.
  • Per-Batch Research Notes: If an entry involves a Real Correction (such as court-ordered release of frozen NIH research funds or an official reversal), the full search trail is documented in a research brief file.tn

Stage 3: Schema Validation & Domain Integrity

The updates are written programmatically, validating against the schema file:tn

  • primary_proponent: Validates that a named individual and their specific role/action are recorded.
  • estimated_impact.figures[]: Ensures every quantified metric includes explicit attribution to who produced the estimate.
  • Timestamp Rules: Updates last_verified to the entry's verification date while keeping date_added immutable.
  • section_summaries Completeness Gate: Re-runs the validation scripttn to confirm every entry's section_summaries object has non-empty what_changed, estimated_impact, and confidence_note values before publishing.

Stage 4: Two-Repository Publishing & Live Deployment

The sweep concludes by compiling and deploying the public feeds:

  • Private Research Repository:tn Staged, committed with detailed batch metrics, and pushed.
  • Publication Engine:tn Transforms the raw JSON entries into the public government services datasettn and updates the frontend's per-domain filters and labels.
  • Public Web Repository:tn Committed and pushed, instantly publishing the verified updates live to the web portal.tn
